## 🎯 What You're Seeing

- **NIFTY 50**: Real-time price (e.g., `26,131.00`)
- **BANKNIFTY**: Real-time price (e.g., `48,500.00`)
- Color-coded change percentages
- Updates every 2 seconds

### Strategy Cards
- **SCANNING** (Blue Radar): Strategy enabled, no positions
- **DEPLOYED** (Green/Red): Strategy has positions → Shows Live PnL

### Portfolio Positions
- **ALGO** badge (purple): Positions from strategies
- **MANUAL** badge (gray): Manual positions from Kite
